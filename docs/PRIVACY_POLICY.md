# watchudoin? Privacy Policy

Effective: September 25, 2026

This policy is a product-ready draft and requires legal review plus verified company and contact details before public release.

## Information collected

- Account data: email address, authentication identifiers, username, display name, and profile image path if provided.
- User content: moment photos, captions, comments, status text, selected recipients, reactions, and nudges.
- Relationship and safety data: connection requests, invite redemption, blocks, reports, and support requests.
- Device and service data: Expo push tokens, platform/device identifier, notification preferences and delivery status, timestamps, and essential operational logs.

The app does not request precise location, contacts, microphone access, payment information, or advertising identifiers.

## How information is used

We use information to authenticate users, deliver private moments to selected recipients, operate connections and invitations, send notifications users enable, prevent abuse, respond to support and safety reports, and maintain service reliability.

## Service providers and disclosure

Supabase provides authentication, database, Edge Functions, realtime delivery, and private file storage. Expo provides app tooling and push-notification delivery. Firebase Cloud Messaging delivers Android notifications. These providers process data on our behalf. We do not sell personal information or use it for third-party advertising.

Information may be disclosed when required by law, to protect users or the service, or during a corporate transaction subject to appropriate safeguards.

## Security and access

Data is encrypted in transit. Moment photos are stored in a private bucket and downloaded using the signed-in user's authorization. Each download is checked against the current access policy, and the app disables persistent image caching for Moment photos. Database row-level security restricts moments and interactions to authorized participants. No system can guarantee absolute security.

## Retention

Moments expire from active product views after 24 hours. The production cleanup policy is to remove expired photo files, associated moment records, and their comments 30 days after upload. Users can also delete their own comments while they can access the moment. Safety reports may be retained longer where needed to investigate abuse or meet legal obligations. Account deletion removes user-associated data and stored photos as described below.

Photos you choose to save to your device include a small watchudoin? watermark. Copies in your gallery are controlled by you and are not deleted by the app's cloud retention cleanup.

## Your controls

You may edit your profile, manage notification preferences, remove connections, block or unblock people, report content, and delete your account in the app. Account deletion is permanent.
