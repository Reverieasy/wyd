# Play Store Content Rating Notes

Use the questionnaire wording shown in Play Console at submission time. Answer from actual functionality, not the preferred rating.

- The app contains user-generated photos and short text captions.
- Content is shared only with mutually accepted connections; there is no public feed, discovery feed, chat, comments, or follower system.
- Users can send Nudges and emoji reactions.
- Users can report a person or an individual photo Moment and can block another user.
- Terms prohibit harassment, threats, sexual exploitation, illegal content, impersonation, privacy violations, spam, and attempts to bypass safety controls.
- Moderation records are restricted from ordinary users.
- The app does not provide gambling, purchases, location sharing, advertising, or built-in access to unrestricted web content.
- The app does not technically guarantee that all user-uploaded photos are suitable for children.

The final age target, store audience selection, and legal minimum age are unresolved release decisions and must match the final Terms, Privacy Policy, and moderation capacity.
